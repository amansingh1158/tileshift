Delete your TileShift account

TileShift lets you delete all data we store about you — your display name, game scores and player ID — at any time.

What we store

  • Display name — the name you type before appearing on the leaderboard.
  • Game scores — score, mode, best tile and timestamp for each finished game.
  • Anonymous player ID — a random identifier used to credit scores to you. We do not store your email, phone number, contacts or photos.

Data is stored in Firebase (Google) and transmitted over HTTPS. AdMob and Google Play Games may collect data under their own policies.

How to delete (recommended)

In the app, open Settings → Delete my account and data and confirm. This instantly removes your scores and name from the leaderboard and clears your player ID from the device.

If you signed in with Google Play Games, your Play Games profile is managed by Google — delete it in Play Games settings or your Google account privacy controls.

Request deletion another way

If you can't access the app, email us from the email address you normally use, telling us your TileShift display name (if any) and roughly when you played. We process requests within 30 days.
