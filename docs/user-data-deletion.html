TileShift - User Data Deletion

Last updated: August 17, 2026

TileShift stores only the data described in our Privacy Policy — an anonymous player ID, leaderboard scores, and optionally your Facebook display name.

How to request deletion

To delete all of your leaderboard data, send an email from the address associated with your Facebook account to:

privacy@example.com (subject: "TileShift data deletion")

Include your player ID if you know it (shown as "You" on the leaderboard) or your Facebook profile name. We will delete your scores and linked identity from the leaderboard database within 30 days and confirm by email.

Local data

Progress, settings and queued scores stored on your device can be deleted at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings (or the browser's site data).
