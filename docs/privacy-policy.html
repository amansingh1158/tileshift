TileShift Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

What we collect

TileShift stores the following data to run the game and its online leaderboard:

  • A randomly generated anonymous player ID (created by Firebase, no personal details).
  • Your game scores, best tile and game mode for each leaderboard entry.
  • If you choose to sign in with Facebook: your public profile name and profile picture, used only to display your name on the leaderboard.

How we use it

Data is used solely to power the leaderboard feature. We do not sell, rent or share your data with any third party for advertising or any other purpose.

Storage and security

Data is stored in Firebase (Google Cloud) with standard encryption. The game itself stores your local progress and settings only on your device.

Deletion

You can delete your leaderboard data at any time. See our User Data Deletion page.
